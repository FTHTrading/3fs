How it works

  1. Tell us. Who you are, where, how many people, about what you earn. That is usually all it takes.
  2. We check it against the source. Every limit, amount and rule is read from the official table and we show you the page. The AI is never allowed to guess a number.
  3. The AI fills the paperwork. Where a form exists, it is filled from what you told us. You get a packet ready to email or mail. You press send.
  4. The team finishes it. When the AI does not know, it hands your case to a person. You get a case number and we tell you what it costs before anything is charged.

Your documents are fingerprinted on your own device and never leave it without your say. See Privacy.
