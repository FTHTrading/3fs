Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · applies to 3fs.app and every *.3fs.app door

The short version. Most of what you do stays on your device. We do not sell information, run ad trackers or upload your documents. We keep the minimum needed to run Pro, paid calls, affiliate payouts and the cases you send us.

What stays on your device

  • Place searches and the household details you type on a door's map are calculated in your browser.
  • Documents you add to a file are fingerprinted on your device and never uploaded.
  • Your browser remembers your role, your Pro key if you have one, and an affiliate code if you arrived through one (90 days). Clear it anytime in browser settings.

What we receive, and when

  • AI guide. Your message and the on-screen context (place, household size, income) go to our server and Cloudflare Workers AI to write the answer. We do not store the conversation. Please do not enter Social Security, bank or card numbers.
  • Matches and packets. When you ask for matches or download a packet, the facts you entered are sent to make it. We keep a log of the time, the door, the ZIP and whether it succeeded. We keep no copy of the packet.
  • Cases. When you press "Send to the team," we store what you chose to send (door, place, facts, your message, a contact if you gave one) so a person can help you. Kept until the case is closed plus 24 months.
  • Pro. Stripe processes payment. We keep your email, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, status, a one-way hash of your key, and the affiliate code if any. We never see your card number.
  • API, MCP and x402 calls. Time, door, ZIP, success and, for paid calls, the blockchain transaction. Blockchain payments are public by nature.
  • Abuse protection. Short-lived counters keyed by a one-way hash of your IP address, expiring within two days.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare processes standard request data to deliver and secure the sites.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We don't use protected characteristics for anything. Where a program itself keys on age, veteran status or disability, that fact is sent only in the match request you trigger and is not logged.

Who we share with

Only the providers that run 3FS: Cloudflare (hosting, database, AI), Stripe (payments) and Coinbase (USDC settlement for agents). We may disclose information if the law requires it. We never send your information to an agency, lender, charity or partner without your explicit consent.

How long we keep it

Pro records while active and up to 7 years after, for tax and accounting. Call logs up to 24 months. Cases until closed plus 24 months. Abuse counters within two days.

Your choices and rights

Ask us to show, correct or delete your information at kevan@unykorn.org. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, and we honor them. We won't treat you differently for using them.

Children

3FS is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes · Contact

If we change this policy we update the date above and notify Pro customers of material changes. UnyKorn LLC · 5655 Peachtree Pkwy NW, Norcross, GA 30099 · kevan@unykorn.org
